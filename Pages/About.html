CH Creations of WNC, LLC:


Jewelry making started 15 years ago, when my oldest daughter assisted with the Secret Sisters of the church as a project. With the knowledge from her and me wanting to be creatative, since my job wasn't, I started CH Creations after a move to a new community. I felt lonely and needed away to socialize in the new community. I began to create and sell at craft fairs on the weekends.

Please see upcoming events page for upcoming shows. Necklaces and bracelets will be added soon! So come back to check them out!
